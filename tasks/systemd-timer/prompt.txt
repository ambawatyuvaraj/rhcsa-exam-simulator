Create a systemd timer that runs a oneshot service every 15 minutes:

  - A service unit {{NAME}}.service of Type=oneshot that runs:
        /usr/bin/logger tmr
  - A timer unit {{NAME}}.timer that triggers it every 15 minutes
    (OnUnitActiveSec=15min or OnCalendar).

Place the units under /etc/systemd/system, then enable the timer so it is
active and starts at boot.

create a logical volume

create a new logical volume according to the following requirements:

  -> The logical volume is named wshare and belongs to the wgroup volume
     group and has a size of 100 extents.
  -> The wgroup volume group should have an extent size of 8 MiB.
  -> Format the new logical volume with the vfat filesystem.

Note: the logical volume should be automatically mounted under /mnt/wshare
at system boot time.
